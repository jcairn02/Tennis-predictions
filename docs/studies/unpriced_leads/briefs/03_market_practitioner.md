## Your role: B3, the market practitioner

Role name for your files: `market_practitioner` (memo `docs/studies/unpriced_leads/team/market_practitioner.md`, CSV `docs/studies/unpriced_leads/team/market_practitioner_ideas.csv`, id prefix `MP`, scratch `data/studies/unpriced_leads/team/market_practitioner/`). You may use the web (WebSearch, WebFetch) as well as the repository.

**Your lens: the 2025–26 closing line as a product of information flow and money.** You think like a professional tennis trader. Which information reaches BetOnline's and Pinnacle's tennis closes late or never, and which crowd errors survive to the close? Then turn each into a statistic the project can compute.

Questions to work through:
* **How the close is formed.** Openings set by a model (rankings, Elo-like ratings) and copied across books; moved by limited sharp money; BetOnline's recreational clientele and its copying of Pinnacle; lower limits on women's, early-round and 250-level matches; overnight versus match-day moves. Where does that process leave blind spots? Read `docs/studies/betonline_lines/`, `odds_sources/`, `bet_timing/`, `sharp_benchmark/`, `line_sharpening/`, `polymarket_sharp_money/` and `docs/research/market-anchoring/`.
* **Biases a close can keep:** favourite–longshot (closed for weak pairs: `pair_strength`), reputation and fame, nationality and home crowds, scorelines and recency (B-OVERREACT exists), retirements and injury returns, qualifiers and lucky losers, young risers and fallen stars against the ranking's lag, lower-level arrivals (U-330: under-rated, growing to 2024), women's volatility, best of five.
* **Information that arrives late or is unwatched:** order of play and time slot (night sessions), weather and heat, altitude, balls, court speed at particular editions, entry and withdrawal news, motivation (ranking points to defend, Slam and Finals cut-offs, national team selection), coaching and equipment changes, doubles entries, practice-court reports.
* **The market's own traces as signals:** does the close under-react to its own open-to-close move (line momentum)? Do the books' disagreements carry direction, given that the target here is the sharp close itself, not the books' average (market-internal inputs U-002–U-007 help only against a soft benchmark)? Polymarket's early flows from accounts that beat its close (2025–26 only: no history to discover on, so say how such a lead could still be tested).
* **What sharp tennis bettors are known to use**, from practitioner writing and your knowledge; label provider claims.

For each idea give the statistic (definition, data, timing class), the mechanism, why it would survive to the 2025–26 close at BetOnline and Pinnacle, how to tell "learned by the close" (an early-bet edge) from "never priced" (an edge at any time) with BetOnline's opening and closing lines (`data/studies/bet_timing/frame.parquet`, 2018–2026), and what would kill it. Separate what can be discovered on seasons ≤ 2024 (needs history) from what exists only in 2025–26.
