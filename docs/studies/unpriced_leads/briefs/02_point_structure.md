## Your role: B2, the point-structure analyst

Role name for your files: `point_structure` (memo `docs/studies/unpriced_leads/team/point_structure.md`, CSV `docs/studies/unpriced_leads/team/point_structure_ideas.csv`, id prefix `PS`, scratch `data/studies/unpriced_leads/team/point_structure/`).

**Your lens: how points, games and sets are won, and where the scoring structure lets results drift away from underlying quality.** The model already holds per-statistic rates (opponent-adjusted, long and short memory: see the shared context). The owner's thesis is that the edge lies in point-by-point and per-statistic offence/defence, not win/loss ratings. The other LLM's advice is "returning vs serving and break-point luck". Your job is information the rates themselves do not carry.

Angles to consider, then go beyond them:
* **Residuals between the levels of the scoring hierarchy** (points → games → sets → matches). With an iid point model (Klaassen and Magnus), a player's serve and return point rates imply how many break points he should save and convert, how many tiebreaks, games, sets and matches he should win. The residuals (actual minus expected) are partly skill and largely luck. The market reads results, scorelines and rankings, which carry the luck; a player whose results ran ahead of his points may be over-rated. Build them separately: break points saved and converted beyond expectation, tiebreaks, games won beyond points, sets beyond games, close sets lost, deciding sets. Stage 0 has every count needed (`tb_won`, `sets_won`, `games_won`, `bp_*`, `spw`, `rpw`). The pool's conversion luck (I-025, inside B-OVERREACT, U-277–U-281) exists: read its definition and say what is new (separate components, the point model as the expectation, shrinkage, all levels, long memory).
* **Serving against returning:** edge_anatomy found, for men in 2025–26 only, that strong servers are over-rated and strong returners under-rated (weakly present before). Mark any idea motivated by this as exposed. What new statistic would capture the asymmetry rather than restate the IOS profile? Examples to weigh: how a player's return holds up against servers stronger than his usual opponents; the balance of a player's game (serve share of his points won); return points won against the server's own norm (careful: the IOS defence side is already opponent-adjusted); second-serve return aggression.
* **Point-level state against the player's own normal** (a state, like the speed signal, not a trait): first serves in, double faults, second-serve points won, unforced errors (WTA advanced statistics), serve speed of the second serve and of faults, speed fade within a match, in the last match or two against his long-run normal.
* **Consistency and volatility:** the spread of a player's match-level dominance, how often he loses points-dominant matches or wins points-losing ones.
* **What scorelines show the market:** flattering scorelines, comebacks, saved match points, bagels (I-086 exists; say what is new).
* **Partial point and tracking data:** Court Vision tracking 2021–24 (serve placement, return depth, rally speed), SlamTracker distance run, WTA advanced statistics 2021–26, the Match Charting Project. Say what coverage a statistic would have in 2013–24 (for discovery) and in 2025–26 (for the test), and what would have to be downloaded or decrypted to fill it.

**Respect what is closed.** point_sequence closed momentum, streaks, big-point and leverage *traits*, and tempo, against exact within-set shuffles and the close; shot_level found 0 of 280 shot-level rows beyond the close; big-point return skill (B-CLUTCH) did not replicate. So "clutch as a skill" is dead. "Clutch results as luck the market pays for" (the reverse direction) is not yet tested as such. A re-proposal must name the new angle: a residual instead of a raw rate, a state against the player's own normal instead of a trait, or the luck-regression direction.

Rank your ideas by your judgment of (a) a plausible effect beyond the 2025–26 close, (b) a mechanism the market would miss, (c) data in hand or cheaply obtainable, (d) availability at bet time. Give the exact formula for your top ideas (the expectation model, the window, the shrinkage).
