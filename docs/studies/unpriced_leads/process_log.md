## 3. Plan (as first written, 13 Oct 2026 07:50, before any result)

**Round 1, launched together in the background.** Briefs verbatim in [briefs/](briefs/) (`00_shared_context.md` + the role file):
* **B1, rhythm and body scout:** readiness, fatigue, rust, adaptation, travel, rest in hours, heat, doubles and qualifying load, health signals, age against the player's own curve — beyond the model's `sets_14d`, `surf_season`, `surf_layoff`, `age`.
* **B2, point-structure analyst:** serve against return; conversion and "luck" residuals (break points, tiebreaks, sets and games beyond what points won imply); point-level state against the player's own normal; serve-speed extensions; partial point and tracking data.
* **B3, market practitioner:** what the 2025–26 close is slow to absorb or never absorbs, and why; information that arrives late; persistent biases; cheap partial information; each turned into a computable statistic.
* **R1, researcher:** the published evidence for predictors beyond the market or rankings, and the sources (coverage, terms, cost, approval route) for the partial-information candidates.

Each brainstormer writes a memo and an `ideas.csv` with fixed columns. None is given any screen result of this study.

**The lead, meanwhile:** writes the shared discovery harness, `src/eda/unpriced_leads/screen.py`, which reads outcomes only from matches before 1 Jan 2025 (filter applied before the result column is read, then asserted), and fits, per era and tour, `logit P(A wins) = s_atp·L·1[men] + s_wta·L·1[women] + β·x` (L = the close's log-odds, x scaled by its RMS, missing = 0, cluster-robust z over four-week blocks), as edge_anatomy's scan does; plus the pooled 2013–24 z, sign consistency over the four eras, the same fit beyond the model's own forecast (2020–24), the open-to-close move at BetOnline (2018–24, outcome-free) and the R² with the 56 non-price inputs (outcome-free).

**S1, the pool statistician** (launched once the harness exists): every built feature-store column not in the model, screened on ≤ 2024 with the harness; redundancy; a family-level summary.

**Synthesis 1 (lead):** merge the ideas into one long list (`longlist.csv`), deduplicate against the model, the pool and the closed ideas, and shortlist what can be built now.

**Round 2: S2a, S2b**, the builders: build the shortlisted new statistics that are safe by construction (they follow each player's own matches in order) from Stage 0 and the other tables on disk, and screen them with the harness. Cross-player statistics only with the match clock and `probe.py`, else flagged.

**Synthesis 2:** choose five groups by these principles (fixed now, before any screen result; guidance, not rules):
1. **New:** not an in-model input or a re-encoding (R² with the 56 non-price inputs below about 0.5, outcome-free).
2. **A mechanism** for why the close would miss it (unwatched data, slow anchors, structural change, crowd over-reaction a close keeps, luck read as skill, …).
3. **Earlier-data evidence** on ≤ 2024 beyond the close: pooled z and sign consistency over eras; or, for a data-needing group, a stated prior and a feasibility plan.
4. **Testable:** coverage in 2025–26, availability at bet time, and the registered test's resolution.
5. **Diverse:** the five span different areas of the game.
6. **The owner's thesis** (the project memory's "edge thesis": per-statistic and point-level measures first) breaks ties.

**Round 3: R2, the red team**, attacks the five groups and the draft registration (leakage, timing, proxies of in-model inputs, multiplicity, power, exposure to 2025–26 results, availability at bet time) and re-derives selected discovery numbers with its own code.

**Freeze:** `preregistration.md`, its SHA-256 recorded here; README (the plan, for the owner); §§ 7–10 of this log.

**Reading rules** (fixed now). z is cluster-robust over four-week blocks, + = the player higher on the statistic beats the close (or oriented to a declared direction where stated). Discovery readings are descriptive: many are run, so they carry a BH q within each screen as a guide, never verdict words beyond what their intervals show. Never "tie".

**Blinding (hard):** no outcome from 1 Jan 2025 on is read by any part of this study; the harness enforces it; agents are told so. Outcome-free counts on 2025–26 (coverage) are allowed.

## 8. Weak points

* **Threats to validity, most important first:**
  * **Selection.** The groups are the best of about 500 statistics, and every era from 2013 to 2024 helped choose them. The discovery z's (omnibus 8.76) are not forecasts; the registration expects a realisation near 0.45.
  * **Exposure.** G1's break-point idea and G5's lens were prompted by 2025–26 results of in-model statistics, and market_anchoring read a G1 relative on 2019–2026 (36 Look B matches included). The in-window re-projection removes the linear channel; non-linear channels remain, estimated by the red team at most about 0.6 of Look A's SE for G4.
  * **Power.** Look A is small for effects of 0.03–0.05 per SD. Single groups will mostly read "unresolved".
  * **Data drift.** Women's ITF qualifying is missing from 2025; every lower level from June 2026; Flashscore's times from 17 Apr 2026; fame's scale drifts up; doubles play declines among men. The gate and the in-window scaling address the first three and the scale; the definitions do not change.
  * **Retirements** carry part of G1's discovery signal; a completed-matches secondary is registered.
  * **The benchmark.** BetOnline's women's line is soft after Sep 2025; the period secondary separates it.
* **Where a flaw would most likely hide:**
  * the luck builder's 2010–12 recalibration (the tiebreak bias it found);
  * the venue pace's cross-player clock;
  * women's imputed minutes before 2016 (past fatigue);
  * TennisLive's name mapping (doubles, 95.6% of member-rows mapped);
  * the gate's calibration (chosen from observed 2023–26 counts).
* **What a re-design would do:**
  * hold out an era from selection (impossible now: every era chose);
  * a hierarchical shrinkage of member weights;
  * Pinnacle's own 2025–26 closes as the base;
  * a stable-coverage women's lower-level share;
  * register before any discovery and screen once.
* **The one experiment most likely to overturn the plan:** Look A itself. A realisation near 0, or contrary readings, would say these areas are priced or were luck.
