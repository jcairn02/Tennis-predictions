# External evidence consulted

Accessed 2 October 2026. These papers support the limited follow-up hypotheses below; none establishes an edge over the closing line in this project's data. No new sporting or odds dataset was downloaded for this audit.

| Primary source | Evidence used | Limit |
|---|---|---|
| Jacob Gollub, [Forecasting serve performance in professional tennis matches](https://journals.sagepub.com/doi/10.3233/JSA-200345), 2021 | Shrinkage and opponent adjustment address thin and uneven serve/return histories; calibrating point forecasts with Elo improved the evaluated forecasts. | Supports the model design, not an independently demonstrated market edge. The audit uses the public article/abstract. |
| [Analysis and Forecasting of Tennis Matches by using a High Dimensional Dynamic Model](https://doi.org/10.1111/rssa.12464), 2019 | Dynamic player abilities specific to court surfaces were useful in its forecasting exercise. | Motivation for partially pooled player-by-surface serve/return ratings; the proposed point model is an inference, not a result measured by this paper or this audit. |
| [Effects of consecutive days of match play on technical performance in tennis](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/27779438/), 2016 | Seven trained men played four consecutive days of simulated four-hour matches; technical performance deteriorated on some measures. | Small simulated setting. Supports testing actual recovery/load, not a profitable prematch fatigue feature. |
| [Heat stress does not exacerbate tennis-induced alterations in physical performance](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC3995225/), 2014 | Physical performance changes during play recovered within 24 hours in this experiment. | Recovery is context dependent; do not turn a generic workload count into an assumed fatigue penalty. |
| Tennis-Data [data description](https://www.tennis-data.co.uk/data.php) | Provider describes average and bookmaker historical odds, weekly season files, and use restrictions. The existing local Stage 1 notes are the authority for what was actually captured. | Search-index text was accessible; direct fetch returned 403 and the notes URL failed. No newly downloaded quotes were audited. The current description restricts automated/commercial or data-training product uses; the archived research data do not establish permission for a future product. |
