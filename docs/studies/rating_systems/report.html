Summary

What we found

decisionCurrent standing: TrueSkill Through Time is the leading candidate for the base rating

Named by the owner on 3 Oct 2026, it is the present leader, not a final choice (decision record). It has the lowest log loss of the 9 ratings on the tuning years, the rule that picks the leader, on both tours. After them it stays best for the men, ahead of the correlated-surface Glicko by +0.54 (−0.02 to +1.10) milli-log-loss in 2019–2026. For the women it is level with TrueSkill with correlated surfaces, −0.29 (−1.29 to +0.71). It makes a better forecast but is not an edge on the market.

The owner asked whether a Glicko-2 or TrueSkill rating, or whatever rating the published evidence finds most predictive in tennis, improves on the surface Elo the earlier studies used as their base, and whether any rating adds to the bookmakers' closing prices. Each system was built for tennis under the same time rule as Elo (a match sees only earlier weeks and earlier rounds of its own event), tuned on the same seasons (2013–2018, with 2010–2012 as warm-up), frozen, and scored on the same 155,130 men's and 60,321 women's matches of 2013–2026. Scores are log loss: lower is better, a coin scores 0.693, and one milli-log-loss is 0.001. The ranges are 95% intervals clustered by four-week calendar blocks, as the shared test now computes them.

observedAgainst surface Elo

On every evaluation match after the tuning years (2019–2026), the leading system of those carried through every test beats surface Elo by +2.65 (+1.89 to +3.40) milli-log-loss for the men (TrueSkill Through Time) and +3.00 (+1.67 to +4.33) for the women (TrueSkill Through Time). Glicko-2 run as Glickman specifies it, a week as the rating period, scores −1.42 and −0.32 against Elo.

observedAgainst the bookmakers

On the main-tour matches with odds (2019–2026) the leading system is +2.28 (+0.86 to +3.69) (men) and +3.01 (+1.28 to +4.74) (women) against Elo. Added to the closing prices through the shared test, it gains +0.04 (−0.02 to +0.11) and −0.02 (−0.08 to +0.04) milli-log-loss.

observedBeside the promising features

In the audit's full feature model (2022–2026), the leading system in Elo's place changes the forecast by +0.48 (+0.03 to +0.93) (men) and +0.19 (−0.56 to +0.94) (women); kept beside Elo it adds +1.17 (+0.70 to +1.65) and +1.21 (+0.32 to +2.10).

What the published evidence says

docs/research/rating-systems

  • Reported. No peer-reviewed tennis study compares Glicko-2 with Elo. Glicko-1 ties Elo in the two peer-reviewed tennis tests (ATP 2018–19; WTA 2022), and TrueSkill ties or slightly beats a constant-K Elo.
  • Reported. The largest published gain over Elo comes from smoothing (TrueSkill Through Time, KickScore): about 0.011 nats per match, but against a constant-K Elo without a surface model, and never against odds.
  • Reported. A surface model is the most consistent extension. No study shows any rating beating the bookmakers, and none tests a rating against closing odds.

The rating systems, tuned and frozen

Each system was built from wins and losses alone, over every singles match from 2010 at every level, under the earlier studies' time rule. Its constants were chosen on the log loss of the overall rating over the evaluation matches of 2013–2018, exactly as Stage 1 tuned Elo; the weight of the surface rating was chosen next. Where a best value lay on an edge of its grid, the grid was widened past it. Everything was then frozen.

Checks

  • The time rule. A probe cut by date, event and round redrew every result each cut may not see and re-ran the pass (96 cuts over 8 systems): 0 of 4,979,008 protected pre-match values moved. A deliberately leaky pass moved 969.
  • Tuning against the frozen pass. Every tuning pass equals the frozen full pass's first rows (largest difference 0).
  • The formulas (test/rating_systems/): Glickman's worked Glicko-2 example and his Glicko-1 example, TrueSkill's update against the exact posterior integrated numerically, Landfried and Mocskos's published TrueSkill Through Time example, the correlated-surface model reducing to TrueSkill and to Glicko-1 at ρ = 1, and every pre-match value of random histories against a fresh pass over only what the rule lets it see.
