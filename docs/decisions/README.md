| 9 Oct 2026 | [Adoption by kind of change](2026-10-09-adoption-by-change-class.md) (the owner, on Claude's recommendation: "I accept your adoptation logic, implement it"). A change is classified before any result is read: accretion, repair, expansion, new feature, structural, tuning. A before/after comparison decides only where it can see the change's plausible effect; below that, the kind's expected direction decides and the reading is a harm check (worse by more than 0.10 mLL with the whole interval below 0). Recurring kinds are decided once as policies; the frozen reference becomes a holdback for the sum of small changes; readings are pooled by kind. **New features go to the pool** (the feature universe) **and selection rounds decide by cluster**; a feature study ends with an admission verdict. Live fits will grow within the season. The level-evidence study's decision rules were replaced before any model-level score existed. Reporting labels unchanged, and only reporting. **[Errata](2026-10-09-adoption-by-change-class-errata.md)** (same night, after an independent review): the holdback's control is the latest frozen instance carrying every structural change (today the Pinnacle-first instance), since a comparison across the odds change resolves only ±0.3 (**approved by the owner the same night**); repairs are not vetoed by a visible reading; costly expansions pay for themselves; the share arms of the level-evidence study are report-only |
