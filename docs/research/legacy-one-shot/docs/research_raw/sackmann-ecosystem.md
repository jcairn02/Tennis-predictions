### NonCommercial clause meaning (high) **[decision-critical]**
The CC BY-NC-SA 4.0 deed defines a commercial use as one 'primarily intended for commercial advantage or monetary compensation'; the NC restriction attaches to reproducing/sharing/adapting the licensed material. Whether a private for-profit betting model counts as 'commercial' is legally unsettled — profit-seeking use arguably qualifies even without redistribution, while purely factual data may have thin copyright protection in the first place; no authoritative answer exists for this dataset.

Source: https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by-nc-sa/4.0/

### Walkover/retirement/default encoding (high) **[decision-critical]**
Walkovers appear as rows in the per-year match files with the literal string 'W/O' in the score column and empty stat fields (verified: all 20 W/O rows in atp_matches_2023.csv have blank w_ace), retirements are completed-set scores suffixed 'RET' (76 rows in 2023), and 'DEF' means default/disqualification (1 row in 2023); Sackmann confirmed 'w/o = walkover, def = default' in issue #120 (2020-01-29). A betting pipeline must drop W/O rows from training labels (most books void bets on walkovers) and decide a policy for RET rows, and a historical quirk of leading spaces in ' W/O' was fixed via issue #64.

Source: https://github.com/JeffSackmann/tennis_atp/issues/120

### Davis Cup data quirks (high) **[decision-critical]**
Davis Cup (tourney_level 'D') rows are mixed into the regular per-year ATP files (220 rows in 2023; 252 in 2025) and are the main source of blank surfaces: in atp_matches_2023.csv ALL 53 blank-surface rows are Davis Cup rows (~24% of that year's Davis Cup rows). Sackmann himself maintains open issues for missing Davis Cup ties (March 2022, issue #164), missing 2022 Davis Cup stats (#208), and missing 2016 Davis Cup results (#229) — Davis Cup coverage is patchy and should be excluded or special-cased in features and surface-conditioned ratings.

Source: https://github.com/JeffSackmann/tennis_atp/issues?q=davis+cup

Source: https://github.com/JeffSackmann/tennis_atp/issues

### Cross-source name normalization (medium) **[decision-critical]**
Sackmann files key players by numeric player_id with full names ('Roger Federer'), while tennis-data.co.uk — the standard free source of historical tennis betting odds (Bet365/Pinnacle/average/best closing prices) — uses abbreviated 'Federer R.' style names (format documented in the welo package manual); there is no official crosswalk between the two, so every odds-joined pipeline re-implements fuzzy name+date+tournament matching (e.g. carltoews/tennis merge_datasets.ipynb provides a worked one-to-one merge of the two sources).

Source: https://github.com/carltoews/tennis

### Match Charting Project selection bias (medium)
MCP matches are chosen by volunteers, so coverage skews toward famous matches and top players: a 2015 Stats-on-the-T analysis found 80% of the first ~1,100 charted matches came from just 5 contributors (479 by Sackmann himself) and warned of systematic bias in which matches get covered; usable for player-level serve/return tendency priors on elite players, but not as an unbiased tour-wide sample for model training.

Source: http://on-the-t.com/2015/09/19/charting-match-charting/

### deuce R package (high)
skoval/deuce (Stephanie Kovalchik) is a GitHub-only R package (install_github, never on CRAN) bundling ~274MB of Sackmann-derived datasets (ATP/WTA matches, rankings, Elo ratings, slam point-by-point) plus functions like elo_prediction and fetch_activity; it received data refreshes on 2025-07-27 and 2025-08-18 ('Update to atp_elo and wta_elo') after a 2.5-year gap (previous update Feb-Mar 2023, 'Version 1.4 with data update thru 2022') — a sporadically refreshed snapshot useful for backtesting, not a live feed.

Source: https://api.github.com/repos/skoval/deuce/commits?per_page=5

### welo R package (high)
welo (Vincenzo Candila, CRAN v0.1.4 published 2024-03-19, GPL-3, still active — manual re-rendered 2026-05-08) implements standard Elo and Weighted Elo per Angelini, Candila & De Angelis 2022 (European Journal of Operational Research 297(1):120-132): K-factor per Kovalchik 2016 of 250/(N+5)^0.4 with optional 1.1x multipliers for Grand Slams or a chosen surface, margin-of-victory weighting by games or sets won, bootstrap confidence intervals on ratings, and betting()/random_betting() backtest functions that bet $1 when model-probability divided by Bet365-implied probability exceeds a threshold r, reporting ROI with bootstrap confidence intervals.

Source: https://cran.r-project.org/web/packages/welo/index.html

### welo data dependency = tennis-data.co.uk (high) **[decision-critical]**
welo does NOT consume Sackmann CSVs: its tennis_data(YEAR, Circuit) function downloads ATP/WTA season files from tennis-data.co.uk (years 2013+ only), and its clean() function expects that site's format — dropping uncompleted matches, rows with missing Bet365 odds/rank/games/sets, and matches where both Bet365 odds are equal — meaning the only turnkey odds-aware open tennis betting tool runs entirely off tennis-data.co.uk's odds-bearing files (which include Pinnacle and Bet365 closing prices).

Source: https://cran.r-project.org/web/packages/welo/welo.pdf

### Python equivalents (medium)
No maintained Python package wraps Sackmann data as of June 2026. The closest tools are both dormant: skelo (PyPI v0.1.5, released 2022-12-02) provides scikit-learn-compatible Elo and Glicko2 estimators whose documentation examples train directly on Sackmann's ATP match CSVs, and tennisim (PyPI v0.1.3, 2022-01-12, github.com/mjam03/tennisim) simulates tennis points/games/sets/matches from serve-win probabilities in pure Python; everything else found is one-off research repo code (e.g. jgollub1/tennis_match_prediction), not a package — so the bettor should plan to write his own loader, as he did for UFC.

Source: https://pypi.org/project/skelo/

## Gaps noticed by the research agent
- Nobody has an authoritative answer on whether CC BY-NC-SA 4.0 'NonCommercial' forbids a private for-profit betting pipeline — the one direct question to Sackmann (tennis_atp issue #269, March 2026) sits unanswered, and every betting project just uses the data silently; a one-line email to Sackmann could resolve what no blog post does.
- No open-source point-level or serve-stat feed exists for CURRENT (2025-2026) tennis beyond match-level aggregates: slam point-by-point repo dead since Oct 2024 (and missing Australian Open/French Open since 2023), tennis_pointbypoint dead since 2017, Match Charting Project biased and sparse — recent serve/return form features must be built from Sackmann's match-level serve totals or bought from a commercial API (Sportradar/OpenDataFeeds-class), and nobody documents this trade-off for bettors.
- No maintained crosswalk maps Sackmann player_id to tennis-data.co.uk names (or to any odds feed's IDs); every project re-implements fuzzy name+date joins from scratch, and the failure modes (father/son pairs like the Damm case, brothers, transliterations) match exactly the duplicate-identity issues open in the repo. A published mapping table would be the highest-leverage missing artifact in this ecosystem.
- Nobody quantifies maintainer-risk: the 16-month 2025 hiatus proved a single-maintainer repo can silently stall mid-season, yet no tutorial tells you to wire a fallback results scraper (TML database, official ATP/WTA sites) from day one; pipelines written before 2025 all assumed evergreen weekly updates.
- WTA data quality is far less audited than ATP: serve-stat coverage by era (0% in 1999 to 95%+ only after 2016, measured here), qual_itf completeness, and the admitted missing-ITF-match backlog have no published audit equivalent to the many ATP analyses — a women's-tennis model needs its own validation pass, analogous to how the user's UFC pipeline trains women's models separately.
- There is no pip-installable, maintained Python equivalent of deuce/welo (Sackmann loader + tennis Elo with documented walkover/Davis-Cup/retirement handling); skelo and tennisim are dormant since 2022. Building one privately is straightforward but the canonical-handling conventions (drop W/O? keep RET as wins? exclude level 'D'?) are folklore scattered across issues and blog posts, never specified in one place.
- Sackmann's data contains no odds whatsoever, and tennis-data.co.uk (the default free odds join) carries only Bet365/Pinnacle/average/max closing-ish prices with no timestamps — no open source provides opening-to-closing line movement for tennis, so closing-line-value tracking like the user's UFC setup requires capturing live Pinnacle quotes himself from day one.

### NonCommercial clause meaning — vote 1 — **confirmed**
Corrected/current fact: Confirmed and current as of June 2026, with refinements. (1) The CC BY-NC-SA 4.0 deed does state verbatim: "A commercial use is one primarily intended for commercial advantage or monetary compensation"; the binding legal code (Section 1) wording is slightly fuller: "NonCommercial means not primarily intended for or directed towards commercial advantage or monetary compensation." (2) The NC limit attaches to the licensed rights in Section 2(a)(1): to "reproduce and Share the Licensed Material... for NonCommercial purposes only" and to "produce, reproduce, and Share Adapted Material for NonCommercial purposes only" — note that private copying/adaptation IS reproduction, so a non-redistributing betting model still exercises licensed rights unless no copyright is implicated or an exception (e.g., fair use) applies (Sections 2(a)(2) and 8(a) expressly disclaim restricting uses that need no permission). Section 4 also limits database extraction/reuse to NonCommercial purposes where EU/UK sui generis database rights exist — so "facts are unprotected" is mainly a US point. (3) The "legally unsettled" characterization matches Creative Commons' own guidance: NC "turns on the use, not the identity of the reuser," gray areas and interpretation debates are acknowledged, facts themselves are not copyrightable ("Facts are not subject to copyright" — CC Data FAQ), and no court or authority has ruled on private for-profit model training under this license; CC's suggested resolution in close cases is to ask the licensor (the tennis_atp README confirms the dataset is CC BY-NC-SA 4.0, "Attribution is required. Non-commercial use only.").
