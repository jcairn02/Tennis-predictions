## 1. The request

* **The owner's words, verbatim** (21 Oct 2026):
  > "Scour the internet to find free 2026 pinnacle tennis odds, we need to fill in that missing gap for our data model"
* **How it was read:**
  * "That missing gap" is § 3 of the [Pinnacle-first decision](../../decisions/2026-10-09-pinnacle-first-odds-input.md): Pinnacle's own closes are missing from August 2025 (tennis-data's PSW/PSL stop being Pinnacle's close that month and stop altogether on 13–14 Jan 2026; OddsTrader's recording of Pinnacle, book 20, stops on 7 Sep 2025). BetOnline's close stands in for those rows. The decision says: if the missing closes are obtained, retrain on Pinnacle throughout.
  * So the target is **Pinnacle's closing match-winner price** (both sides) for the model's main-tour singles, ATP and WTA. The owner names 2026; the gap starts in August 2025, so the search covers August 2025 to now, with 2026 first.
  * **"Free"** = publicly served without payment. The [scraping approval](../../decisions/2026-10-08-scraping-approved.md) covers publicly served data and the decryption a page performs, not others' logins, paywalls or CAPTCHAs. A free tier that needs an account or API key in the owner's name is reported, not signed up for: creating an account is an outward-facing act to confirm first.
  * Set aside: opening or early lines (not asked; the bet-timing study found no gain from training on them); paid archives (BettingIsCool, The Odds API: already listed by the [early-lines note](../early-lines/README.md)); the live recorder (exists: `src/ingest/pinnacle.py`, bet-day pulls since 11 Oct 2026).
* **The target:** not a model-scoring study. The use is the training odds input (`odds_logit`) for the frame's matches from August 2025, replacing BetOnline's stand-ins. No result of any model is read here.

## 8. Weak points

* **The site's last price is not a timed close.** It comes a median 42–65 minutes before the listed start; matches that start late are priced earlier still. The odds study found the model inherits its input's softness; a slightly early close may cost a little accuracy. Unmeasured here (the accuracy reading was deferred with the test).
* **Untimed rows in August – October 2025:** whose price it is, and at what time, is unknown. Not used by the rule.
* **The guards catch gross errors only** (over 1.0 in log-odds and 10 points). A smaller recording error on the site passes.
* **The second guard trusts BetOnline and the average when they agree.** If both carry the same wrong price (both from OddsTrader-like sources linked the same wrong way), the site's correct price would be dropped. That falls back to BetOnline, the status quo.
* **The links use names and dates.** All 216 second-pass links of the gap and all 37 of the first read were checked by eye for the first 1,000 pages only. The 216 of the full gap were not all read.
* **The site's terms** were not recorded. Read under the owner's scraping decision.
* **Where a flaw would most likely hide:** in months the samples did not cover closely (December, team-event weeks), and in the site changing its page layout (the Finals and the week under way each needed a parser fix).
* **A re-design** would read the site's prices against Pinnacle's own closes on all of January – July 2025 (the overlap, running), and against our own recorder near the start of many matches. Then, with the owner, the deferred accuracy reading against BetOnline's close.
