       starting lanes 1 and 2. It must end before lane 1 reaches leakproof_frame (about 90 min into lane 1); if lane 1
       gets there first, the guard raises and run_chain --lane 1 --from leakproof_frame resumes once the IOS part is done.
    2. lanes 1 and 2 (beside each other), lane 3, collect: unchanged.
    Guards: lane 1's leakproof_frame checks the IOS part and the merge; lane 3's surface_ios_arms checks lane 2 too. Each
    compares the fingerprint the hooked step recorded with the arm config's. A config with both IOS families off uses the
    stored tables, which are the reference's, and passes.
    Lane 2 writes its runs to surface_ios/full_runs/lle_<name>_<fingerprint>/, so one sandbox can build several arms.
