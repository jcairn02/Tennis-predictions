SIGNATURE (src/eda/lower_level_evidence/classify.py)
    gamma(through, tours=("atp", "wta"), stats=None, start="2011-01-01",
          stage0_dir=common.STAGE0_DIR, timing=common.TIMING,
          strength_path=classify.STRENGTH, allow_holdout=False) -> pandas.DataFrame
    season_cutoff(season) -> pandas.Timestamp     # earliest possible start (es) of season Y's first main-tour match

WHAT IT FITS
    Per (tour, base statistic): y = logit((x + 1/2) / (d + 1)) per main-tour main-draw box-score player-match
    (log scale for bp_faced_pg and def_bp_faced_pg; the builders' scale), regressed on the OPPONENT's pre-match Elo / 100,
    with player x season fixed effects and surface dummies, weighted by the units d; 95% intervals clustered by player.
    Rows: bucket MAIN, completed, standard format, box score, tournament_date >= start, and lf + 1 <= through (the match
    clock: a result is in at lf + 1). gamma for season Y: gamma(season_cutoff(Y)).
    Stats (classify.STATS): ace df first_in first_won second_won spw hold bp_saved bp_faced_pg (serve);
    rpw rpw_first rpw_second brk bp_conv (return); tpw (none); def_ace def_df def_first_in def_bp_faced_pg (defence:
    what servers do against the player; the regressor is then the server's Elo).
    A statistic with no within-player-season variation in the window (women's holds, breaks and break points per game
    before 2015: no service-game counts) returns gamma NaN instead of failing.

RETURNS one row per (tour, stat): gamma, se, gamma_lo, gamma_hi, gamma_unit ("logit" or "log", per 100 Elo), n_rows,
    n_player_seasons, n_players, partial_r2, sd_match, side, through, start, last_result_day (latest lf + 1 used),
    strength_source, method_version.
    Sign: gamma < 0 means the statistic falls against stronger opponents (e.g. spw -0.060 per 100 Elo, men, through
    2020). A lower-level match against an opponent 200 Elo weaker reads 0.12 logit too high on spw; T subtracts it.

HOLDOUT
    Refuses (PermissionError) when a 2025-2026 outcome would be read. Note: through=2024-12-30 already refuses (9 main-draw
    matches of season-2025 events were played on 29 Dec 2024); season_cutoff(2025) = 2024-12-29 reads none (tested on the
    frozen Stage 0). allow_holdout=True only for the SPEC's mechanical walk-forward fits of seasons 2025 and 2026
    (diagnostics unread).
