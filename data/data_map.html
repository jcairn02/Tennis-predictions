Tennis Data Map

Every professional singles match since 2018 that the files in data/ know about, by tournament level and year: how much detail we hold for each, from every shot of a rally down to just who won; how we got it and from where; and, set against everything known to exist, what we still lack.

Four levels of detail

Each match gets the richest level any file holds for it. Each level contains the ones below it: a shot-by-shot chart also gives every point, the totals and the result. These colours are used everywhere below.

The headline

What we hold, against what exists

For each tour and tournament band, the upper bar is what data/ holds and the lower bar what is known to exist publicly, ours included: sources measured to hold these matches but not collected yet, and files on disk that are still encrypted. Paid feeds are not counted. The figure at the end of each bar is the share with every point or better.

Sources we have not collected

By tournament level and year

Each box is one tournament level in one year. The bar splits its matches by the richest detail; the number under it is how many matches were played. Grey is matches played that no file (or, in the second view, no source) lists. Hover or tab onto a box for the numbers, the files they come from and notes.

Being downloaded right now

What is missing

The biggest holes left in what we hold, the number of matches each covers, and where the data could come from. These are options, not decisions.

How we got it

Each dataset by the way it was obtained, and how much of data/ each way filled (downloaded files and their tables). Every download kept a receipt per file: where from, when, its size and a checksum.

Where the data came from

Every dataset in data/, grouped by the finest detail it reaches. The small columns show how many of the map's matches each one holds per year, 2018 to 2026. Open "Where it is" for the folders, the code that fetched it, the write-up and the rights.

Betting-market data

Not part of the detail ladder, but in data/ too: bookmakers' closing odds, BetOnline's opening and last lines, and Polymarket's tennis markets with minute prices and every trade.

Where it lives in data/

Data moves left to right: downloaded as-is, turned into tables, then checked. The cleaned, joined database is the next step and is not built yet.
